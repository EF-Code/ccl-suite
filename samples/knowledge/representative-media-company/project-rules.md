# Project Aurora Rules

## Access and ownership

- Only the Project Aurora owner, team lead, or administrator may retrieve Project Aurora knowledge sources.
- A request for another project's sources must be denied and recorded as an access-denied security event.
- Knowledge sources must be approved before ingestion, search, or answer use.

## Project records

- Use the project storage folder for scripts, exports, review copies, and
  source records.
- Preserve original assets and version history during organization or restore.
- Archive completed campaign records after the approved retention review.

## Approval

Only the team lead or administrator may approve a knowledge source or an editorial publication decision.
