# Community Management SOP

## Moderation workflow

1. Community managers respond using the approved response tone and escalation
   categories.
2. Hide spam, impersonation attempts, and abusive comments when platform tools permit it.
3. Escalate credible safety threats, legal claims, or account-access reports to the team lead.
4. Do not request passwords, recovery codes, payment details, or private contact information through comments or direct messages.

## Publishing support

- Use the approved caption and publishing window supplied after editorial
  approval.
- Report a broken link or failed post to the assigned project owner.
- Do not publish a draft, unapproved edit, or unreleased campaign material.
